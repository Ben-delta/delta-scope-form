2 · Walk (room by room)

Status and field meanings (official)
  • known: fact, with no assumption in the row. Only a client change order, a newly found source, or a Selection recorded as an upgrade/downgrade from the baseline changes it.
  • assumed: a stated assumption is the placeholder the estimate is written on, e.g. "Assumed 6 mil poly". Reason required. Damage jobs (Ben, Oct 28): like-kind rows are assumed with the basis "like kind and quality" and the existing material written in, e.g. "Assumed: like kind and quality, 3/4" solid oak strip, site-finished". Picking assumed on a damage job pre-fills "Assumed: like kind and quality, " for you to finish.
  • hold: too many variables to assume yet. State exactly what's missing. Flagged but doesn't block the estimate; goes in as a placeholder; clears with information. Reason required.
  • contingent: reasonably expected, but only the work reveals the extent. Excluded from the base total; priced when exposed. Qty may be blank. Reason required (what exposes it). Example: "The subflooring and framing is contingent on if we find damage and the extent of it we find in these areas once the removal of the floors is performed."
  • Material / Item:
  • Finish starts blank on every new entry; it is never copied from another room.
  • Option is not a status: set Selection to "Option: TBD". The row is excluded from the base total.
Rows
  • Start a new row when the Action or the Material changes, not the room. Same item + same action in several rooms = one row (the page combines them).
  • Same material with different quantities, notes or the way it fails = separate entries (e.g. partial baseboard + all quarter round). Same quantity everywhere = one ("baseboard and quarter round").
  • A wrong qty is fine; it changes in the next revision. A range (e.g. 96-128) is only allowed on a hold. Qty must be a number above 0 (contingent may be blank). Typing or tapping is the default. Optional voice (approved: optional input): tap 🎤 next to Qty and say it, e.g. "fourteen square feet" → 14 SF. Nothing listens or converts unless you tap 🎤.
  • Scope says what it is (material, action, quantity of the thing), not what it takes (labor hours, time, cost). Time thoughts go in the purple Estimating note (time): internal estimating aid only, not a schedule commitment, never in the plan set or client exports.
Actions
  • remove only: tear-out, no reinstall (demo = remove only).
  • install only: install new or provided material; no material bought on this row.
  • remove and replace: tear-out plus new install with materials.
  • replace: new install with materials, no tear-out on this row. Paint = replace (Ben, 2026-10-08).
  • detach & reset: take off, put the same piece back. Not new material.
  • clean/treat: Mitigation only (e.g. microbial clean/treat).
  • n/a: General Conditions (GC) only.
Where things go (locked)
  • Floor protection goes under GC, not FLR. Tape, texture and primer stay inside DRY or PNT.
  • Shower drain, faucet, piping and trim go under PLUMB. Waterproof membrane and tile shower system go under TILE.
  • MECH, PLUMB and ELEC: Material is the system or fixture class, not every SKU.
  • If remove and install quantities differ, use two rows.
  • General Conditions: the Material field is the Item: what it is (Supervision, Dumpster, Portable toilet, Permit, Floor protection), not what it takes. No hours or cost in the Item.
  • Not sure? Tick ⚑ Stuck on the entry and type what you're thinking; it saves with the job and gets reviewed.
