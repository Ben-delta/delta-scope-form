2 · Walk (room by room)

Status and field meanings (official)
  • known: fact, with no assumption in the row. Only a client change order, a newly found source, or a Selection recorded as an upgrade/downgrade from the baseline changes it.
  • assumed: a stated assumption is the placeholder the estimate is written on, e.g. "Assumed 6 mil poly". Reason required. Proposed (for Ben's review): on a damage job, like-kind rows are assumed with the basis "like kind and quality" and the existing material written in, e.g. "Assumed: like kind and quality, 3/4" solid oak strip, site-finished". Picking assumed on a damage job pre-fills "Assumed: like kind and quality, " for you to finish.
  • hold: too many variables to assume yet. State exactly what's missing. Flagged but doesn't block the estimate; goes in as a placeholder; clears with information. Reason required.
  • contingent: reasonably expected, but only the work reveals the extent. Excluded from the base total; priced when exposed. Qty may be blank. Reason required (what exposes it).
  • Material / Item:
  • Finish starts blank on every new entry; it is never copied from another room.
  • Option is not a status: set Selection to "Option: TBD". The row is excluded from the base total.
