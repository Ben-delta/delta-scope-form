Rules (read-only)

From the Level 2 Scoping Workbench, Rules tab (locked while we test). The form enforces these; you can't turn them off here.

What this form makes

  • One Level 2 plan-set table. Your sketch, photos and plan set stay outside the form; the table points to them by Callout ID.
  • In damage, the damage defines the scope which defines the price. A damage job needs the inspection report and the damage source(s) first.
  • The Level 2 table is the benchmark of the details as stated today. The estimate is written from it, and the cost budget and RFPs are generated from it. Later findings and selections go into a new version (v2, v3…). The benchmark is never overwritten.
  • Level 2 is complete when the annotated visuals and this table are saved together in the agreed location. The form can only check the table; you confirm the sketch.

Row rules

  1. Trade is a Cost Group from the official code list only. No free-text trades. CAB, CLN, DRY, PNT and the other codes are Delta's Cost Group codes, not Xactimate categories.
  2. Start a new row when the Action or the Material changes. The same Trade can have many rows. "Carpet and pad" is two rows.
  3. Every row has Callout ID, Trade, Action, Material, Location, Qty, Unit, Finish and Status.
  4. Status is known, assumed or hold. Blank is not done.
    • known: solid enough to treat as fact.
    • assumed: filled so work can continue. The note must say what was assumed.
    • hold: can't honestly fill specifics yet. Qty, Unit, Material and Finish can stay soft. Callout, Trade, Action, Location and Status are still required.
  5. Every Callout ID matches exactly one row. One row can carry several IDs.
  6. A wrong Qty is fine (it changes in the next version). A Qty range is only allowed on a hold.
  7. What versus what it takes (Ben, 2026-10-08): Every prompt and preset asks for what something is (the material, the action, the quantity of the thing), not what it takes (labor hours, time, cost). That keeps scope from drifting into estimating.

Actions

  • remove only: tear-out, no reinstall.
  • install only: labor to install new or provided material; no material bought on this row.
  • remove and replace: tear-out plus new install with materials.
  • replace: new install with materials, no tear-out on this row. Paint = replace (a new coat with materials). Decided by Ben, 2026-10-08.
  • detach & reset: take off, put the same piece back. Not new material.
  • n/a: General Conditions (GC) only.

Where things go (locked)

  • Floor protection goes under GC, not FLR. Tape, texture and primer stay inside DRY or PNT.
  • Finish: State the existing quality as the like-kind baseline (the client is owed like-kind). Describe it so a sub could bid it without seeing it: material, color, style, how it's built. No grade words on their own. Later selections are an upgrade or downgrade from this baseline — not open holds. (Ben, 2026-10-08)
  • Selection (optional): the client's choice, kept apart from the baseline. Write "Selection pending client" until they choose, then record the choice as an upgrade or downgrade from the Finish baseline in a new version. Status stays about the baseline, so a pending selection is not a hold.
  • Shower drain, faucet, piping and trim go under PLUMB. Waterproof membrane and tile shower system go under TILE.
  • MECH, PLUMB and ELEC: Material is the system or fixture class, not every SKU.
  • If remove and install quantities differ, use two rows.
  • (v1.5) New line only when the Action or the Material changes, not the room. Same material + same action in several rooms = one line: list the rooms and give one combined Qty.
  • (v1.5) Same material with different quantities (partial baseboard + all quarter round) = split into two lines. Same quantity everywhere = combine ("baseboard and quarter round").
  • (v1.5) General Conditions: the Material field is the Item: what it is (Supervision, Dumpster, Portable toilet, Permit, Floor protection), not what it takes. No hours or cost in the Item. Floor protection and anything else that covers several trades goes under GC.
